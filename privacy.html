Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

Hermes Personal Google Assistant is a private productivity integration operated for the application owner's personal use. It is not offered as a public or commercial service.

Google user data accessed

Depending on permissions granted by the authorized user, the application may access:

  • Gmail messages, message content, headers, labels, and metadata;
  • Google Calendar events and associated event information;
  • Google Drive files and file metadata;
  • Google Contacts information;
  • Google Docs documents; and
  • Google Sheets spreadsheets.

How Google user data is used

Google user data is used only to provide functionality requested by the authorized user. This may include:

  • identifying and summarizing important email;
  • displaying and summarizing calendar events;
  • retrieving user-requested documents or information;
  • producing personal productivity reports and daily briefs; and
  • performing actions explicitly initiated or authorized by the user.

Data processing and disclosure

Information required to perform a requested function may be processed by the AI service configured by the application owner. Generated summaries may be delivered to communication channels configured by the owner, including a private Discord channel.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared for unrelated purposes.

Data storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored on systems controlled by the application owner. Access to those systems is limited to the owner and services configured by the owner.

Application data may be retained only as needed for requested workflows, scheduled summaries, application logs, security, troubleshooting, and operation of the service.

Data retention and deletion

Locally retained application data can be deleted by the application owner. The owner can revoke the application's Google access at any time through Google Account connections. Deletion requests may also be submitted using the contact information below.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Hermes Personal Google Assistant's use and transfer to any other application of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change. The current version will remain available at this URL.
