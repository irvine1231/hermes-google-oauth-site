Hermes Personal Google Assistant

Hermes Personal Google Assistant is a private productivity integration used by its owner. It is not offered as a public application or commercial service.

Purpose

The application connects to Google services to help its authorized user review important email, view calendar events, retrieve user-requested information, and create personal productivity summaries.

Depending on permissions granted by the user, the integration may access Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Contacts, Google Docs, and Google Sheets.

Access

Access is limited to the application owner and any users personally authorized by the owner. The application is not available for general public registration.
